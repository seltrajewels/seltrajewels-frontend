Our story

Made for the moments
in between

Seltra began with a simple belief: beautiful jewellery shouldn’t be saved for special occasions. It should be part of your morning coffee, your workday, your weekend plans, and the celebrations too.

Each piece is chosen for the details that make it feel fine — the glow of 18K gold plating, a finish that resists tarnish, and materials that are kind to your skin — at a price that lets you wear it every day.

Demi-fine sits between fashion and fine jewellery. It has the look and finish of fine pieces, made to be worn every day, at a more accessible price.

Our pieces are 18K gold plated with an anti-tarnish finish, designed to keep their shine with everyday wear.

Put perfume and lotion on before your jewellery, take it off before swimming or showering, wipe it gently with a soft cloth, and store each piece separately to avoid scratches.

Every piece is chosen to be skin friendly for comfortable, all-day wear. Full material details will be listed on each product at launch.

Shipping and return details will be shared at launch. If you have a question before then, we’re happy to help.
